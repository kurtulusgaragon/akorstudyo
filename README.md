# 🎸 AkorStudyo | Guitar Workshop & Pro DAW

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)
[![Offline Ready](https://img.shields.io/badge/Offline-Ready-success.svg)]()

**AkorStudyo** is an advanced, offline-first, browser-based guitar studio and Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) developed by **Garagon**. Designed for musicians to learn, practice, and record their ideas instantly without installing any external software.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [www.akorstudyo.com](https://www.akorstudyo.com)

## ✨ Key Features

* **🎙️ Pro DAW Studio:** Multi-track audio recording directly in the browser. Merge, mixdown, and export your tracks (WAV/MP3).
* **🎸 Interactive Fretboard:** Dynamic visualizer supporting both right-handed and left-handed guitarists.
* **🧭 Chord Compass & Scale Assistant:** Discover new chord progressions and recommended solo scales (Pentatonic, Major, etc.) dynamically based on your root notes.
* **🎛️ Smart Tuner & Metronome:** Highly accurate microphone-based guitar tuner and a feature-rich visual metronome with tap-tempo.
* **💾 Offline-First Architecture:** Powered by IndexedDB and Local Storage. Once loaded, the entire app works flawlessly without an internet connection. No data leaves your device.
* **🌍 Bilingual Support:** Seamless real-time switching between Turkish (TR) and English (EN) interfaces.
* **🎮 Gamification:** Take a coffee break with musical retro mini-games embedded within the studio.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

* **Frontend:** Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
* **Audio Engine:** Web Audio API, lame.js (for MP3 encoding)
* **Database & Storage:** IndexedDB (Local Audio Blobs), LocalStorage (UI Preferences)
* **Analytics/Live Count:** Firebase Realtime Database
* **Hosting:** Netlify
